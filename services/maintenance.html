Maintenance Services

Our maintenance services ensure the longevity and efficiency of your RO systems. We provide routine checks, preventive maintenance, and affordable maintenance packages designed to keep your system running at peak performance year-round.

What Our Maintenance Covers

  • System Inspection – Complete check of all components and connections
  • Filter Status Check – Inspection of all filter stages and replacement if needed
  • Membrane Performance Test – TDS testing before and after membrane
  • Pump Pressure Check – Booster pump pressure calibration and testing
  • Leak Detection – Checking all joints, fittings, and tubes for leaks
  • Tank Inspection – Storage tank pressure and condition check
  • UV Lamp Testing – UV intensity verification and lamp life assessment
  • TDS Controller Calibration – Setting optimal TDS levels for your area
  • System Sanitisation – Internal cleaning and disinfection
