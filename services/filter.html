Filter Servicing

Regular filter servicing is essential for maintaining water quality. Over time, filters accumulate contaminants and lose effectiveness. We provide thorough cleaning and high-quality replacement filters to ensure your RO delivers pure, safe water at all times.

Filters We Service

All Filter Stages Covered

We service, clean and replace every stage of your RO filtration system.

PP Sediment Filter

Removes sand, rust, dirt and visible particles. Replace every 3–6 months.

Pre-Carbon (GAC)

Removes chlorine, bad taste and odour from water.

CTO Block Carbon

Further chlorine and chemical removal before the RO membrane.

RO Membrane

Removes dissolved solids, heavy metals and bacteria. Replace every 12–24 months.

Post Carbon Filter

Final taste and odour polishing for clean, fresh water.

UF Membrane

Ultra-filtration for bacteria and cyst removal.

UV Lamp

Kills remaining bacteria and viruses. Replace every 12 months.

Mineralizer Cartridge

Restores beneficial minerals to purified water.

⚠️
Reduced Flow

Slow output = clogged pre-filters. Get them replaced today.

👃
Bad Taste / Smell

Musty or chlorine smell = carbon filters exhausted.

📊
High TDS Levels

Rising TDS means the RO membrane needs replacing.
